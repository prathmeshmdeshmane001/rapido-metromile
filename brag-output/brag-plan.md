# Brag Plan: Rapido MetroMile (Bengaluru Commute)

## What is this app?
A multi-modal urban transit subscription that pairs guaranteed Rapido bike Captains with Namma Metro's Purple Line on one fixed monthly schedule.

## The angle
In Bengaluru, public transit is fast, but getting to and from the station is an everyday gamble. MetroMile flips on-demand rideshare on its head: instead of scrambling for four rides a day through surge and cancellations, commuters pre-allocate morning and evening connections on a single pass.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An isometric dark Bengaluru landscape zooms in on a single illuminated point: *"Your morning starts with a Captain."* The 8:00 AM booking anxiety disappears before you put your shoes on.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The 3D Transit Corridor**: Elevated Purple Line viaduct connects Kasturi Nagar to Brookefield seamlessly.
2. **The Synchronized Commute**: 08:00 Home pickup → 08:12 Baiyappanahalli Metro → 08:42 Kundalahalli exit handoff.
3. **The Live Delay Sync**: A 12-minute train signal halt dynamically reschedules the destination Captain without missing a beat.
4. **The Captain Win**: Guaranteed recurring shifts with zero deadhead miles.

## Outro / punchline
"Your city. All connected." A complete desk-to-desk routine, locked in for ₹3,200/month.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Selection & Verification**: Home pickup area (Kasturi Nagar) & desk time (09:00 AM) verified within the 2 km station catchment.
2. **Allocated Morning Handoff**: Captain Ravi Kumar assigned with vehicle OTP before departure.
3. **Desk Arrival**: Arrive at office by 08:50 with a 10-minute peace-of-mind buffer.

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm, modern electronic bed with crisp interface feedback.
- Music: `bgm.mp3` (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10`), 110 BPM, starts at 0.0s, settled bed volume ~0.75, subtle fade-out at 18.5s.
- Music cue guidance: Major reveals at 3.0s, 7.3s, 12.0s, 16.3s, and 18.5s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle glow and depth pulse on yellow accent cards aligned to the rhythmic pulse.
- SFX posture: Restrained, tactile UI sounds (`click_001.ogg`, `select_008.ogg`, `switch_001.ogg`).
- Audio-coupled moments: Card reveal on beat, schedule pin lock sound, station arrival chime.
- Restraint rule: No harsh alarms or excessive whooshes; keep the sonic atmosphere premium.
